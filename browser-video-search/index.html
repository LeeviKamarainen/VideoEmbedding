Visual search, in your browser

Every frame and every question is turned into a vector by EmbeddingGemma 2 running on your own GPU (WebGPU), so you can search a video, your photos or your camera by describing what you saw, and ask to be told when something comes into view. Nothing is uploaded: the model downloads once and stays in your browser.

Pick any number of photos: each is embedded here and can then be searched.

Two frames a second are embedded and remembered while the camera runs, so you can search what it saw and be told when something you watch for comes into view. The camera stays on this device.

    Watching works on a playing video and on the live camera: a frame that stands out from the last 20 seconds for what you described sets it off.
